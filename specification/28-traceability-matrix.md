## Orphan policy

Normative requirements, public interfaces, legal state transitions, tests, tasks, and accepted ADRs cannot be orphaned. Pure research sources and explanatory diagrams may be informational, but each declares its role. The cross-artifact validator reports an orphan rather than silently assigning one.
